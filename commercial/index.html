Damage Prevention

Finding and Preventing Water Risks at Your Property

The law expects owners of commercial buildings to keep their properties in safe condition, known as a duty of care. Hidden moisture, aging plumbing, and inadequate waterproofing can result in sudden failures that cause injuries, property damage, and costly lawsuits. Fishers Restoration provides professional water damage risk assessments using moisture monitoring equipment and thermal imaging technology that evaluate every area's vulnerability to water intrusion. Findings are delivered in writing, with risks identified, priorities assigned, and specific prevention measures recommended.

There are two benefits here, since spotting weak points keeps the property and its occupants safer, while documented due diligence may help you legally after an incident. Owners who keep up regular professional inspections and follow the experts' advice are viewed favorably by insurers and courts. Every commercial property should have an annual moisture assessment, and high-risk areas, including aging plumbing systems, basements, and mechanical rooms, should be inspected more often.
