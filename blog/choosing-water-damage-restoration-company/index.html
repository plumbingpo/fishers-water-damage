Choosing a Water Damage Restoration Company in Fishers, IN

Home / Blog / Selecting a Restoration Professional in Fishers, IN

Picking a restoration company is a major decision for any Fishers, IN owner dealing with water damage. That choice affects the speed of the restoration, how completely the damage gets fixed, what comes out of your own pocket, and whether mold or other secondary problems appear months down the road. Sadly, some operators in this industry are not qualified, and they can leave behind unfinished work, padded bills, or worse. Use the points below to tell a strong restoration company apart from a weak one.

Verify IICRC Certification

The Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC) is the gold standard for restoration industry credentials. Technicians who hold it have gone through demanding coursework on the science of water damage restoration: correct extraction, structural drying methods, measuring moisture, and preventing mold. Look for two current IICRC certifications on the crew: WRT (Water Damage Restoration) and ASD (Applied Structural Drying). Steer clear of any firm unable to show proof of them.

Make Sure They Are Insured and Licensed

Workers' compensation and general liability coverage are must-haves for a restoration contractor on your property. Have them send certificates of insurance and confirm the dates are current. Without proper coverage, you could be liable for injuries or additional property damage that occur during the restoration. Also verify that the company holds any business licenses required by Fishers or Indiana regulations.

Check Availability and Arrival Speed

Water damage is a time-sensitive emergency. Each hour lost adds to both the damage and the bill. Real 24/7 emergency service, with crews that reach your neighborhood fast, should be part of the deal. Find out their typical response time for Fishers, and verify the emergency number goes to a real person, since an answering service or voicemail only adds delay.

Ask About Equipment and Process

Without the right machines, a restoration job cannot be done properly. Find out what they extract water with (truck-mounted units beat portable-only setups), which dehumidifiers they run (the industry standard is LGR), and if thermal imaging is used to find moisture. Ask how drying is monitored too, because a reputable company checks moisture every day and repositions equipment according to the readings instead of setting it up and walking away.

Read Reviews and Call References

Online reviews provide valuable insight into a company's track record. Look at the BBB, Yelp, and Google, and watch for steady patterns in quality, professionalism, and how satisfied customers were. Pay particular attention to reviews that mention the company's communication, timeliness, insurance assistance, and the final quality of restoration work. Don't hesitate to ask for references from recent water damage projects in Fishers.

Know How They Price and Bill Insurance

Expect a detailed estimate up front, along with a plain explanation of the pricing, from any firm worth hiring. Good firms often price jobs with industry-standard software such as Xactimate, which insurers know and accept. Find out if they deal with insurance carriers directly and can send the bill straight to your insurer. Be wary of any company that wants a large payment up front or will not work with insurance.
